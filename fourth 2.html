How to Face Cyber Crime

1. Stay Calm

Do not panic. Take a deep breath and carefully assess the situation before reacting.

2. Collect Evidence

Take screenshots, save emails, chats, or messages related to the crime. This will help in investigation.

3. Change Passwords

Immediately change passwords of your accounts, especially email, banking, and social media.

4. Report the Crime

Report the incident to the official cybercrime website (cybercrime.gov.in) or nearby police station.

5. Use Security Tools

Install updated antivirus, firewall, and security patches to protect your device.

6. Seek Support

If affected emotionally or financially, seek support from family, friends, or helpline numbers.
